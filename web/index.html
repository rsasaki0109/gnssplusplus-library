    40 s of the PPC Tokyo run1 drive as RTCM3 (rover 5 Hz, base 1 Hz, GPS) with the reference trajectory and 100 Hz IMU, plus a synthetic PPP set.

    Post-processing

    Input files
    MADOCA L6E logs orbit / clock / bias: every channel and hour of the run
    MADOCA L6D logs optional: ionosphere over Japan and Australia

    Galileo HAS gives float PPP (no phase biases, so no ambiguity resolution) with GPS and Galileo; use the broadcast NAV of the same day, with Galileo I/NAV. East Asia and the Pacific (90°E–180°E) are outside its service area.

    BeiDou PPP-B2b gives float PPP with GPS and BDS-3 over China and the Asia-Pacific. The NAV must be RINEX 4 with BeiDou B-CNAV1 (CNV1) records of the same time, such as the receiver's own; add an ANTEX file for the BDS antenna offsets.

    QZSS L6 logs are the 250-byte frames of an L6 receiver or the hourly files of the QZSS archive; use the broadcast NAV of the same day.
